Memory and Encoding Experiment

You will study 18 concrete words in three blocks. Each block uses a different way of encoding the words. Please follow the instructions for each block carefully.

Your data stay in this browser. No server or database is used.

Distractor task

For 30 seconds, count backward by 3s from the number below. Type each number you produce, separated by spaces or commas.

30

The task will end automatically when the timer reaches 0.

Free recall

Type as many of the 18 studied words as you can remember, in any order. Separate words with spaces, commas, or new lines. Do not worry about capitalization.

Results

Scoring ignores capitalization and accepts small spelling deviations when they are sufficiently close to a target word. Each target word can receive credit at most once.
